How it works

From report to resolution in three steps

No paperwork, no chasing staff. Every issue gets a tracking ID and a clear owner.

01

Report

Choose a category and location, describe the problem and optionally attach a photo.

02

Track

Use your private tracking ID to see the current status and every update from staff.

03

Resolve

Maintenance teams prioritise, act and record remarks until the issue is closed.
